## Scene 4: Complete Worked Mathematical Example
**Duration:** 2:00 – 3:30

| Time | Visual | Subtitle |
|------|--------|----------|
| 2:00–2:08 | "Finding the Derivative" heading, f(x)=x², "Find f′(2)" | "Let us calculate the derivative of f(x) = x² at x = 2." |
| 2:08–2:20 | Limit definition written out | "We start with the limit definition of the derivative." |
| 2:20–2:25 | — | "f(2+h) is the output at the nearby point; dividing by h gives the average rate." |
| 2:25–2:38 | Substitution step | "Substitute f(x) = x² into the formula." |
| 2:38–2:50 | Expansion: 4+4h+h²−4 | "Expand (2+h)² to get 4 + 4h + h²." |
| 2:50–3:02 | Simplification: (4h+h²)/h | "The 4 and −4 cancel, leaving (4h + h²) / h." |
| 3:02–3:16 | Factor h, cancel, get (4+h) | "Factor h from the numerator. Since h ≠ 0 during the limit, we cancel h." |
| 3:16–3:30 | Result: f′(2) = 4, highlighted box | "Let h go to zero. The derivative f′(2) equals 4." |
